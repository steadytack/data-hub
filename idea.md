# Data Hub (Trusted Data Products)

## The idea
Put the supplied Trusted Data Products specification through the factory: a platform that publishes BUUK's data as versioned, documented products that people and AI tools can find, query and rely on, with the trust tier and known issues travelling with every answer. The first build is the Phase 0 prototype from Build Brief 001: the Environment Agency's Historic Flood Map from download to a published product, plus a synthetic sites product for a cross-product spatial join.
