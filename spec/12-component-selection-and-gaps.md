## Rejected candidates
- **CAT-057 dagster and CAT-059 dagster-postgres:** approved and working elsewhere, but the source reverses the Dagster decision in favour of Airflow.
- **CAT-054 dbt-duckdb:** dbt on DuckDB, not Postgres.
- **CAT-004 etl-pipeline (and its modules):** its runner and SQLite store are replaced by Airflow and dbt; only the hash-and-skip idea is borrowed.
- **PrefectHQ/fastmcp** *(starred)*: compared in CAP-006; not needed beside the official SDK.
- **OpenMetadata and DataHub as the catalogue:** several services including a search engine on one host; the spike decides (ASM-011).

## Known composition limitations
- The five imported libraries were approved on a static review and an install check; their own test suites have not run.
- Airflow, Cosmos and dbt working together has been shown only as installs and imports, not as a DAG run; that is EPIC-005's first test.
- dbt Core 1.x is used while dbt v2 is announced as the default for new installations (RISK-008).
- Phase 2 and 3 components (CMP-011, CMP-014 to CMP-018) are not chosen or proven; each is decided before its epic.
